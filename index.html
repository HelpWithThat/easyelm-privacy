Easy Elm Remote Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Easy Elm Remote (by Easy Elm) turns your Android phone into a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard. It is built to respect your privacy: Easy Elm Remote does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

What Easy Elm Remote does not do

  • It has no internet permission and cannot connect to the internet.
  • It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • It sends nothing to the developer (Easy Elm) or to any third party.

Permissions and why they're needed

  • Bluetooth (connect / advertise): only used to pair with and connect to your own computer or tablet, and to send the mouse movements and keystrokes you make. These go directly to your paired device over Bluetooth.
  • Microphone: only used while you tap the mic button for voice typing. Easy Elm Remote passes the audio to your phone's built-in speech recognition service (for example, Google's speech service). That service turns the audio into text, and Easy Elm Remote types the text on your connected device. Easy Elm Remote itself does not record, save, or transmit audio. The speech service may process audio under its own privacy policy, and depending on your phone's settings that processing may happen on the device or online.
  • Notifications / foreground service: shows the "connected" notification so the Bluetooth link stays active while the screen is off.
  • Vibration: haptic feedback on key presses and gestures.

Data stored on your phone

Easy Elm Remote keeps your settings (theme, sizes, saved PC names and Bluetooth addresses) only in its private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. App backups are disabled.

Children

Easy Elm Remote collects no data from anyone, including children.
